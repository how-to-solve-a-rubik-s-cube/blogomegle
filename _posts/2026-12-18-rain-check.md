The company canceled the walking tour at eight in the morning by text, which is a poor hour to receive a shrug. Daniel stood in the lobby with a day bag and the text. He was forty-three. Catarina arrived to tell the three people who had not read it. She was thirty-nine, the guide, and she had the look of a person whose schedule had been torn by someone at a desk.

"You get a refund," she said. "I am sorry. The other guide is ill and they would rather cancel than pay me double, which is a sentence I am not supposed to say. I am saying it because the rain is already here and I am annoyed."

"Daniel. Keep the sentence. I did not come for a performance of a city. I came because I had a free day and a bad habit of filling it. If you are also free and also annoyed, I will buy lunch. No tour voice. Thirty-nine is fine. Forty-three is what I am."

She considered the offer for the length of a practical thought. The other two travelers had already left for a museum. The lobby was theirs.

"Lunch," Catarina said. "And then I am going to look at a staircase I like and you may come if you can be quiet in the good parts. I am off the clock. That means I can leave."

"You can leave," Daniel said. "I know."

They ate grilled fish while the rain tried the windows. Catarina talked about the company's wages without turning Daniel into a tribunal. He listened, asked one useful question, and did not offer to write a review as if reviews were justice. He was a structural engineer on holiday from a project that had used him up. She was a guide because she liked walking and history and disliked offices, and the disliking had a price. He did not romanticize the price. That was the moment she decided he could see the staircase.

The staircase was in a building that did not advertise. They were quiet in the good parts. After, the rain had settled into a pace they could join. Daniel changed his flight home by two days only after he had asked her whether a second lunch would be welcome or would crowd her week. She said one lunch was a sample and a second was a decision. They made the decision. The company never rebooked the tour. Catarina still walks for them on other days, and she is looking at a different employer, a fact Daniel supports without managing. He has gone back to his project and returned twice, tickets he pays for, days she chooses.

A rain check, in the old phrase, is a delay. Theirs was a cancellation that refused to waste the day. The staircase is still there. So is the fish. So are two adults who can leave when they want, and who, more often now, stay.

**End.**
