Omegle sites: which name is which

Nov 18, 2026 · 5 min read

In short

These names get typed in the same search, and they are not the same product. omegle.com is no longer the 2009–2023 stranger chat. omeglechat.love is the chat site: one stranger, text, voice, or video, no signup. omeglechat.online is the older address for that room. omezy.net is our orange sister brand, with a topic word before you match. talzy.com is not a chat site at all. Adults only.

People still type Omegle when they want a stranger and a Next button. The results mix a closed product, a renamed chapter, sister brands, and companies that only sound nearby. Read the address before you allow a camera.

omegle.com

Omegle began in 2009 as the simple door: one stranger, text or video, then next. That chapter closed in November 2023. The founder shut the original service down.

The address is up again, and the page calls itself a new chapter. The rooms on it now are public and topical. You join a moderated group around a film, music, language practice, or an everyday interest. That is a shared room, not a private 1-to-1, and it is not OmegleChat. If you wanted the old rhythm, omegle.com is not the login that still runs it.

omeglechat.love

This is the chat site this blog is for. OmegleChat is free random chat in the browser. You meet one person. Text keeps the camera off. Voice is the microphone only. Video is face to face. Each mode is its own queue, so choosing text does not drop you into a camera chat.

There is no account in front of the first message. You confirm you are 18. Worldwide is the fastest queue. Same country and other countries are there if you want them. Report and block sit on the session. The rules are short: adults only, no nudity, no harassment, a live person, private 1-to-1.

It is not a dating app. There is no profile and no promise that the other person wants a relationship. It is also not the old Omegle company.

omeglechat.online

omeglechat.online is the older address. People still remember it and still search it. The room they want is the one on omeglechat.love.

A familiar domain is not a guarantee. Bookmarks go stale. Some results send you to a host you did not choose. Look at the name in the address bar before you allow a camera. Use HTTPS, a visible Next button, and a report control. If a page asks you to download a file, hand over a phone number, or pay before any message, leave.

omezy.net

Omezy is our orange sister brand, on its own site. The idea is the same family: one stranger, in the browser, text or voice or video, and you can skip. The difference is the front door. You can add one topic word you would actually talk about, plus a region, before you enter the queue. If nobody with that interest appears quickly, the search widens.

You can start as a guest. A registered account is marked on Omezy as coming soon, for longer sessions and saved interests. Omezy is not Omegle, and it is not a separate company from this brand family. Chat on this domain still starts at omeglechat.love. Interest matching starts at omezy.net.

talzy.com

Talzy is not an Omegle site. It does not match strangers, and it does not run a video chat. Talzy builds dedicated engineering teams in Europe, mainly Latvia, Lithuania, and Poland, for companies that want to hire there. The pages are about salaries, offices, and employment.

The names sit near each other in a search box. That is the only link. If you opened Talzy looking for a stranger, you are on the wrong site. Go back to the address you meant to open.

Other names people still line up

OmeTV, Monkey, and Chatroulette are the brands most often placed next to Omegle. They are not operated by OmegleChat.

OmeTV is a video-first stranger chat, with text as well, in the browser and in an app. Monkey is a social video app. It is app-first and expects an account. Chatroulette is the early random-video name. The format people associate with it is video, not a separate text room.

None of them is the old Omegle company. None of them is Omezy. A longer comparison, including what is live and what is not, is on Omegle vs OmeTV, Monkey, and Chatroulette.

One more trap is worth naming. A download that calls itself an Omegle APK, from a result you do not recognize, is a different product. OmegleChat has nothing to install.

How to pick

Open the site you chose, then read the first screen.

  • You want one stranger, and you want the camera off until you decide. Start on omeglechat.love and pick Text.
  • You want a topic word before the queue. Read Omezy, then decide there.
  • You want a moderated group around a film or a language. That is the new chapter on omegle.com, not this room.
  • You want to hire engineers in Europe. That is Talzy, and it is unrelated.
  • You want a specific other brand’s app. Go to that brand’s own site. Do not install a file from a lookalike.

If the chat feels wrong, leave. Next is one tap. Report is there for harassment, sexual content you did not want, spam, or someone who appears underage.
